# Prescriptions & Antibiotics

## Can MDLIVE doctors prescribe antibiotics?

Yes. Doctors can prescribe antibiotics for bacterial infections like strep throat, urinary tract infections, and skin infections when medically appropriate.

## What's the difference between viral and bacterial infections?

Viral infections (colds, flu) don't respond to antibiotics and usually resolve on their own. Bacterial infections (strep throat, UTI) may require antibiotics. Your doctor will determine which type you have.

## Does MDLIVE prescribe controlled substances?

MDLIVE does not prescribe controlled substances (narcotic painkillers, stimulants, benzodiazepines, opioids) for new patients. Existing patients established with a provider may be eligible.

## How long does antibiotic treatment last?

Most courses are 7-10 days. It's important to take the full course even if you feel better, to prevent antibiotic resistance. Never skip doses or stop early.

## What antifungal medications can be prescribed?

Doctors can prescribe topical antifungals (creams, powders) and oral antifungals for fungal infections like athlete's foot, ringworm, yeast infections, and nail infections.

## Are there any common allergies or side effects with antibiotics?

Penicillin and sulfa allergies are common. Antibiotics can cause stomach upset, yeast infections, or allergic reactions. Tell your doctor about previous adverse reactions to antibiotics.
