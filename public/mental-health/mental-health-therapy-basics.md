# Mental Health Therapy Basics

## What should I expect from my first therapy session?

Your therapist will listen to your concerns, ask about your background and symptoms, and work with you to identify goals. You'll learn coping strategies and develop a treatment plan together.

## How much does therapy cost through MDLIVE?

Cost varies by insurance coverage. Many major insurance plans cover mental health visits. Uninsured patients can see pricing upfront. Therapy can often be canceled with 24 hours notice for a full refund.

## Can I get medication through MDLIVE Mental Health?

Yes. Psychiatrists can prescribe psychiatric medications if medically appropriate. MDLIVE does not prescribe controlled substances (stimulants, benzodiazepines, opioids) for initial visits.

## How often should I have therapy sessions?

Most people benefit from weekly 30-50 minute sessions. Your therapist will recommend a schedule based on your needs. Sessions can be adjusted as your progress.

## Can MDLIVE therapists provide disability paperwork?

No. MDLIVE therapists do not complete outside paperwork or provide formal written recommendations. Consult your primary care doctor or seek an in-person evaluation for official documentation.

## What conditions can MDLIVE Mental Health treat?

Therapists and psychiatrists treat depression, anxiety, ADHD, bipolar disorder, OCD, PTSD, relationship issues, stress management, and many other mental health conditions.
