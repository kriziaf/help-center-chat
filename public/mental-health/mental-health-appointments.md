# Mental Health Appointments & Provider Selection

## When are mental health appointments available?

MDLIVE provides fast and easy scheduling with convenient times including evenings and weekends. Have your first therapy appointment in a week or less, compared to weeks or months for in-person visits.

## What is the difference between a therapist and a psychiatrist?

Therapists are licensed providers who evaluate and support people with emotional or behavioral health concerns through talk therapy. Psychiatrists are medical doctors who primarily diagnose psychiatric conditions and can prescribe medication.

## Do I get to choose my therapist or psychiatrist?

Yes, you always choose your therapist or doctor and can see the same one each time. MDLIVE provides a broad network of licensed therapists and board-certified psychiatrists. Our directory gives additional information about each provider to help you find the right fit. You can change at any time.

## What are the most common issues therapists care for?

Stress, anxiety, depression, and trauma are the most common. However, MDLIVE licensed therapists are trained to provide support for many mental health needs.
