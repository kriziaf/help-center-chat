# Dermatology Warts & Fungal Infections

## Can MDLIVE treat fungal nail infections?

Yes. Dermatologists can diagnose fungal nail infections via photo submission and prescribe oral or topical antifungal medications. Treatment may take several months as the nail gradually grows out.

## What fungal skin infections can MDLIVE treat?

MDLIVE dermatologists treat ringworm, athlete's foot, jock itch, and other common fungal infections. Photos help determine the type and severity.

## How are warts treated through telehealth?

Board-certified dermatologists can recommend topical wart treatments (salicylic acid, cantharidin) or discuss other options like cryotherapy referrals based on wart location and type.

## Are antifungal prescriptions covered by insurance?

Many antifungal medications are covered, especially generic options. MDLIVE can provide cost estimates before treatment begins.

## Can I get prescription antifungal creams?

Yes. For fungal skin infections, dermatologists can prescribe topical antifungals like terbinafine, clotrimazole, or miconazole depending on the type of infection.

## How long does fungal infection treatment take?

Skin fungal infections typically clear in 2-4 weeks with treatment. Nail infections require 6-12 months of treatment as the infected nail grows out.
