# Dermatology Care Through Telehealth

## How does telehealth for dermatology work?

You upload clear photos of your condition with a detailed description, then a board-certified dermatologist reviews and responds — typically within 24 hours. You can ask follow-up questions within 3 days.

## What is a MDLIVE Dermatology visit?

Dermatology is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year in most states. Dermatology is a consultation visit, not an interactive visit. You submit photos and a description for the dermatologist to review.

## What can my MDLIVE Dermatologist treat?

MD Live dermatologists treat 3,000+ skin conditions, from acne and eczema to rashes. Upload photos via the app or website for evaluation.

## What if a dermatologist needs to see me in person?

In some cases a dermatologist may refer to an in-office visit for biopsy, procedure, or closer evaluation. The MD Live dermatologist will guide you on next steps.

## Do I need a referral for dermatology?

No. You can start a dermatology visit right away without a referral. Your appointment will be reviewed by a board-certified dermatologist. In some instances the dermatologist may recommend a follow-up with your primary care doctor.
