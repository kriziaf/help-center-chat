# Dermatology Acne Treatment

## Can MDLIVE dermatologists treat acne?

Yes. Board-certified dermatologists at MDLIVE treat various types of acne including hormonal, bacterial, and cystic acne. Consultations are conducted via video or phone with photo submission.

## How do I get an acne diagnosis through telehealth?

Send clear photos of the affected areas from different angles. Your dermatologist will assess the type and severity of acne and recommend treatment options.

## What acne treatments does MDLIVE offer?

Treatment options include topical creams (benzoyl peroxide, retinoids, antibiotics), oral medications (antibiotics, hormonal treatments, isotretinoin), and skincare recommendations.

## Can I get prescribed isotretinoin (Accutane) through MDLIVE?

Isotretinoin requires close medical monitoring and an in-person visit to establish baseline labs and ongoing monitoring. MDLIVE dermatologists can evaluate whether you're a candidate and refer you to a local dermatologist if appropriate.

## How long does acne treatment take to work?

Most topical treatments show results in 4-8 weeks. Oral medications may take 8-12 weeks. Consistency with treatment is important for best results.

## Are acne prescriptions covered by insurance?

Many acne treatments are covered by insurance. Generic options are often available. MDLIVE can help estimate out-of-pocket costs before treatment.
