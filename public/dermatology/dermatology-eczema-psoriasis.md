# Dermatology Eczema & Psoriasis

## Can MDLIVE treat eczema?

Yes. Board-certified dermatologists can diagnose and treat various types of eczema including atopic dermatitis, contact dermatitis, and seborrheic dermatitis via telehealth consultation.

## How is eczema diagnosed through photos?

Your dermatologist will examine clear photos of affected areas, ask about symptoms (itching, burning, dryness), triggers, and medical history to diagnose and treat eczema.

## What eczema treatments are available?

Treatment options include topical corticosteroids, emollients, topical calcineurin inhibitors, and systemic medications for severe cases. Skincare recommendations are also provided.

## Can MDLIVE treat psoriasis?

Yes. MDLIVE dermatologists can treat plaque psoriasis, guttate psoriasis, and other psoriasis types. Treatment depends on severity and affected body surface area.

## What psoriasis treatments does MDLIVE offer?

Options include topical treatments (corticosteroids, vitamin D analogs, coal tar), phototherapy recommendations, and referrals to specialists for biologic medications if needed.

## How do I manage eczema or psoriasis long-term?

Your dermatologist will provide a maintenance plan including skincare routine, trigger identification, and follow-up visits to monitor treatment effectiveness and adjust therapy as needed.
