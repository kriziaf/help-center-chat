# Men's Health Overview

## What men's health services does MDLIVE offer?

MDLIVE offers treatment for erectile dysfunction, hair loss, low testosterone, prostate concerns, sexual health, and general men's primary care. Board-certified physicians provide confidential telehealth consultations 24/7.

## Can I get treatment for erectile dysfunction?

Yes. MDLIVE doctors can evaluate erectile dysfunction and prescribe treatment options based on your medical history. Consultations are completely confidential and conducted via video or phone.

## Are visits covered by insurance?

Many major insurance plans cover MDLIVE men's health visits. Copays vary by plan. Uninsured patients can see cost estimates upfront before scheduling.

## Can I get a prescription for hair loss or low testosterone?

Yes. If medically appropriate, MDLIVE doctors can prescribe treatments for hair loss (finasteride, minoxidil) and low testosterone after reviewing your health history and symptoms. Prescriptions are sent to your preferred pharmacy.

## How confidential are men's health visits?

Completely confidential. All men's health consultations are private, encrypted, and covered under HIPAA protections. Your information is never shared without your consent.

## Can I follow up with my regular doctor?

Yes. Upon request, MDLIVE can share visit notes and treatment plans with your primary care doctor to ensure continuity of care.
