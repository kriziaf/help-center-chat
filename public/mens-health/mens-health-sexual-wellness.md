# Men's Health Sexual Wellness

## What treatment options are available for erectile dysfunction?

MDLIVE doctors can prescribe FDA-approved medications for erectile dysfunction such as sildenafil (Viagra), tadalafil (Cialis), or other options based on your medical history and preferences.

## Is erectile dysfunction treatment confidential?

Completely. MDLIVE visits are private and encrypted. You can discuss sexual health concerns without judgment in a confidential environment.

## Can I get a same-day appointment for sexual health concerns?

Yes. MDLIVE offers on-demand visits for sexual health issues. You can often connect with a doctor within minutes during business hours.

## Are there any health conditions that prevent ED treatment?

Your MDLIVE doctor will review your complete health history, current medications, and heart health to ensure treatment is safe. Some conditions or drug interactions may require alternative approaches.

## How long does ED medication take to work?

Most ED medications take effect within 15-60 minutes. Your doctor will explain what to expect and how to use the medication safely and effectively.
