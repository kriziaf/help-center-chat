# Men's Health Prescriptions

## Can MDLIVE doctors prescribe finasteride for hair loss?

Yes. If appropriate for your condition, MDLIVE doctors can prescribe finasteride (Propecia) to treat male pattern baldness. The medication works best when started early and taken consistently.

## What is minoxidil and can I get it through MDLIVE?

Minoxidil (Rogaine) is a topical treatment for hair loss. MDLIVE doctors can prescribe it for male pattern baldness as a standalone treatment or in combination with finasteride for better results.

## Can I get testosterone replacement therapy?

Yes. MDLIVE doctors can evaluate symptoms of low testosterone and prescribe TRT if appropriate. Treatment options include injections, gels, patches, or pellets. Regular monitoring is required.

## How do I refill my men's health prescriptions?

You can request refills through the MDLIVE app or portal. For ongoing treatments, your doctor may set up automatic renewals. Prescriptions are sent directly to your pharmacy.

## Are men's health medications covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by insurance plan. MDLIVE can help you understand your coverage before prescribing. Many treatments have generic alternatives that are often covered.
