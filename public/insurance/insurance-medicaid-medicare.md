# Insurance Medicaid & Medicare

## Does MDLIVE accept Medicaid?

MDLIVE accepts Medicaid in participating states. Coverage and copay amounts vary by state and plan. Check your state's Medicaid program for specific MDLIVE coverage details.

## Which states cover MDLIVE through Medicaid?

Coverage varies by state. Contact your state Medicaid office or check MDLIVE's state coverage map to see if telehealth is covered in your location.

## Does MDLIVE accept Medicare?

Yes. MDLIVE accepts Medicare for eligible patients. Some services may require in-person visits under Medicare rules. Copays or coinsurance may apply depending on your coverage.

## What's covered under Medicare for telehealth?

Medicare covers telehealth visits for a wide range of services. Your responsibility depends on your specific plan (Original Medicare, Advantage plan, etc.). Check your coverage details.

## Are there age restrictions for Medicare patients?

Medicare is available to people 65+ and some younger individuals with disabilities or specific conditions. MDLIVE serves Medicare patients in all eligible age groups.

## How do I update my insurance information in MDLIVE?

Log into your account and update your insurance details in settings. Changes take effect for your next visit. Updated insurance is applied to existing appointments if timing allows.
