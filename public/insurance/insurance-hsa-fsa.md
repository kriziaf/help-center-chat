# Insurance HSA & FSA

## Can I use my HSA to pay for MDLIVE?

Yes. MDLIVE is an eligible medical expense under IRS guidelines. You can use your Health Savings Account to pay for telehealth visits and some other services.

## What's the difference between an HSA and FSA?

HSA is a Health Savings Account with no yearly limit, rollover of unused funds, and investment options. FSA is a Flexible Spending Account with a yearly limit ($3,200 in 2024) and "use it or lose it" rules.

## Can I use my FSA for MDLIVE visits?

Yes. Telehealth visits are typically eligible FSA expenses. Confirm with your FSA plan administrator that MDLIVE qualifies under your specific plan.

## Are prescription medications covered by HSA/FSA?

Yes. Prescription medications are eligible HSA and FSA expenses. Over-the-counter medications may be eligible depending on your plan (prescription-strength items typically qualify).

## How do I pay MDLIVE with my HSA or FSA card?

During checkout, select HSA or FSA as your payment method and enter your card information. The payment processes like any other card payment.

## What documentation do I need for HSA/FSA reimbursement?

Keep receipts and explanation of benefits from MDLIVE. Your FSA/HSA provider may ask for proof of the medical service. MDLIVE can provide documentation if needed.
