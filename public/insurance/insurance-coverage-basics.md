# Insurance Coverage Basics

## What insurance does MDLIVE accept?

MDLIVE accepts most major insurance plans including Aetna, Anthem, Blue Cross/Blue Shield, Cigna, Humana, UnitedHealthcare, and many others. Check our website for your specific plan.

## How do I know if my insurance covers MDLIVE?

Enter your insurance information during signup to check coverage. You'll see your copay amount (if applicable) or confirmation that services are covered at no cost.

## What are typical copays for MDLIVE visits?

Copays vary by insurance plan, ranging from $0 to $50+ depending on your coverage level. Some plans cover telehealth visits completely. MDLIVE shows pricing upfront before you book.

## What if I don't have insurance?

Uninsured patients can pay out-of-pocket. MDLIVE provides transparent pricing, typically $59-$99 per visit depending on the type of visit. Payment plans may be available.

## Can I use HSA or FSA to pay for MDLIVE?

Yes. Most Health Savings Accounts and Flexible Spending Accounts cover MDLIVE visits. Check with your account administrator for specific coverage details.

## How do I submit claims if my insurance didn't pay?

MDLIVE handles insurance claims processing. If your claim is denied, MDLIVE can help you file an appeal. Keep copies of your visit receipts and explanation of benefits.
