# Pediatric Care Vaccines & Wellness

## Can MDLIVE pediatricians provide vaccines?

MDLIVE can evaluate vaccine needs and provide prescriptions or referrals to local pediatricians or pharmacies for vaccination. Some vaccines may need to be given in-person.

## What's the recommended vaccination schedule for children?

MDLIVE follows CDC guidelines for childhood vaccinations including DTaP, MMR, polio, hepatitis, and others. Your pediatrician can review which vaccines are due at your child's age.

## Can I get a wellness visit for school or sports physicals?

Some wellness visits can be conducted via telehealth. However, sports physicals may require in-person examination and signature. MDLIVE can assess whether your child can have a telehealth visit.

## Does MDLIVE provide well-child checkups?

Yes. Pediatricians can conduct well-child visits via video, discussing growth, development, nutrition, and answering parenting questions. Complete physical exams may require in-person follow-up.

## Can MDLIVE assess developmental delays?

Pediatricians can screen for developmental concerns by asking about milestones (speech, motor skills, social development). More extensive evaluation may require in-person specialist assessment.

## What preventive care does MDLIVE offer children?

Services include developmental screening, nutrition counseling, behavior guidance, and recommendations for exercise and screen time. Regular preventive visits help catch health issues early.
