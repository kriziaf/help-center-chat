# Pediatric Care Behavioral & Mental Health

## Can MDLIVE help with childhood anxiety or depression?

Yes. MDLIVE offers mental health services for children including therapy and psychiatry consultations. Pediatricians can also screen for and discuss mental health concerns.

## Does MDLIVE treat childhood ADHD?

Yes. Pediatricians and psychiatrists can evaluate ADHD symptoms and prescribe medications if appropriate. ADHD management often includes behavioral strategies and regular monitoring.

## What ADHD medications can be prescribed?

Common options include stimulants (methylphenidate, amphetamine) and non-stimulants (atomoxetine, guanfacine). Medication choice depends on your child's age, symptoms, and medical history.

## Can therapy help behavioral issues in children?

Yes. MDLIVE offers behavioral health counseling for children struggling with behavior, emotions, social skills, or family conflict. Therapists work with parents and children together.

## How does MDLIVE approach childhood behavioral management?

Providers discuss behavior triggers, teach coping strategies, and provide parenting guidance. Behavior improvement often requires consistency and regular follow-up visits.

## Are child mental health visits confidential?

Yes, with limitations. Parents typically participate in sessions with younger children. Older children may have more privacy, but serious safety concerns must be reported per legal requirements.
