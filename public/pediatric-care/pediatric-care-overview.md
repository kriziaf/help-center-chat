# Pediatric Care Overview & Eligibility

## Are pediatricians available for MDLIVE visits?

Yes. MDLIVE board-certified pediatricians are available in nearly every state. If a pediatrician is not available in your state, you can choose from family practice doctors who specialize in caring for children.

## Which age groups can MDLIVE provide care for?

MDLIVE visits are available to children of all ages, from infants to teenagers.

## Are there restrictions on what MDLIVE pediatricians can care for?

MDLIVE does not provide care for children: under 2 months with eye issues, under 3 months with a fever, under 3 with ear pain, under 12 with cold sores, under 18 with a UTI (females only), under 18 for virtual primary care. Cannot prescribe antibiotics for ear infections in children under 12. Sophie, the MDLIVE chatbot, will guide you through triage before requesting a visit.

## What are the most common conditions treated by MDLIVE pediatricians?

Our doctors treat more than 100,000 children every year and can help with 80+ common conditions including: allergies, cold and flu, ear pain, headache, insect bites, pink eye, prescriptions, rash, sinus problems, sore throat, and more.

## Can a doctor accurately diagnose and treat my child through a telehealth visit?

Yes. Using a five-part formula: Clinical Guidelines, Sophie, Temperature, Images, and Specially Trained Providers. While many conditions can be cared for by phone or video, your provider may recommend in-person care based on symptoms.

## Can we see an MDLIVE doctor after hours for my child?

Yes. Pediatricians and family medicine doctors are available 24/7 on weekdays, weekends, and holidays. There are no extra fees for after-hours visits.
