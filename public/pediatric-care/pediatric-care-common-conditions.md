# Pediatric Care Common Conditions

## What childhood conditions can MDLIVE pediatricians treat?

MDLIVE pediatricians treat cold and flu, ear infections, sore throat, rashes, pink eye, stomach upset, fever, urinary tract infections, and other common pediatric conditions.

## Can MDLIVE pediatricians diagnose ear infections?

Yes. Pediatricians can assess ear infection symptoms via video visit. If diagnosis is uncertain, they may recommend an in-person visit for otoscope examination with your local provider.

## Can my child get a strep throat diagnosis through telehealth?

Pediatricians can assess symptoms like sore throat, fever, and swollen lymph nodes. If strep is suspected, they may recommend a rapid strep test at an urgent care or recommend treatment based on clinical judgment.

## How are childhood rashes evaluated?

Clear photos of the rash from different angles help pediatricians diagnose conditions like hand-foot-mouth disease, chickenpox, eczema, or heat rash. They'll also ask about symptoms and exposure history.

## Can MDLIVE treat chronic childhood conditions?

Yes. Pediatricians manage chronic conditions like asthma, allergies, eczema, and ADHD. Ongoing treatment requires regular visits and monitoring.

## Are pediatric prescriptions sent to my pharmacy?

Yes. Prescriptions for children's medications are sent directly to your preferred pharmacy. Your pediatrician will provide dosing instructions based on your child's age and weight.
