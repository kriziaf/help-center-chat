# Pediatric Care Allergies & Asthma

## Can MDLIVE pediatricians diagnose and treat allergies?

Yes. Pediatricians can assess allergy symptoms and prescribe antihistamines and other allergy medications. Allergy testing may require referral to a specialist.

## What allergy medications can be prescribed for children?

Pediatricians can prescribe age-appropriate antihistamines (cetirizine, loratadine), nasal sprays, and other medications based on your child's age and allergy symptoms.

## How is childhood asthma managed through telehealth?

Pediatricians review asthma triggers, symptoms, and current medications. They can adjust treatment plans, prescribe rescue and controller inhalers, and recommend when to seek emergency care.

## What should I do during an asthma attack?

Use your child's rescue inhaler (albuterol). Seek emergency care if breathing doesn't improve or if your child has severe difficulty breathing, chest pain, or confusion. Always have an action plan from your pediatrician.

## Can MDLIVE refill my child's asthma or allergy medications?

Yes. Pediatricians can refill maintenance medications like controller inhalers and allergy medicines. Request refills through the app or portal.

## How often should my asthmatic child see a pediatrician?

Children with mild asthma need periodic check-ups. Those with moderate to severe asthma need more frequent monitoring. Your pediatrician will recommend the best schedule.
