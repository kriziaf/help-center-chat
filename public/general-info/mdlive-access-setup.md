# MDLIVE Access & Setup

## What devices can I use to access MDLIVE?

You can access MDLIVE via computer, smartphone, or tablet using a web browser or mobile app. Make sure your device has a camera and microphone for video visits.

## What are the system requirements for MDLIVE?

You'll need an internet connection, a device with a camera and microphone, and a supported web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge). Mobile apps are available for iOS and Android.

## Is MDLIVE accessible for people with disabilities?

Yes. MDLIVE is committed to accessibility and complies with WCAG 2.1 AA standards. Our platform supports screen readers, keyboard navigation, and captions for video visits. Contact support if you need specific accommodations.
