# MDLIVE Service Basics

## What is MDLIVE?

MDLIVE offers reliable 24/7 health care by phone or video. Our national network of board-certified doctors, pediatricians, dermatologists, psychiatrists, and therapists provides personalized care for hundreds of medical and mental health needs. No surprise costs. No hassle. MDLIVE has two NCQA certifications and ATA accreditation. 9 out of 10 patients would recommend MDLIVE.

## When is MDLIVE available?

Urgent Care doctors are available 24/7/365 for on-demand care. Scheduled appointments (therapy, primary care) are subject to provider availability. Evening and weekend times are typically available for all services.

## How much does it cost to use MDLIVE?

Creating an account is free. If you have MDLIVE from your health plan or employer, appointments could cost as little as $0 depending on benefits. You are only charged after scheduling and confirming payment. MDLIVE accepts most major credit and debit cards.

## Is MDLIVE safe and private?

Yes. Confidentiality is part of the MDLIVE Code of Ethics. Laws including the HIPAA Privacy Rule protect your privacy and personal health information. MDLIVE can share info with your PCP in accordance with applicable laws.

## How do I create an account?

Go to the MDLIVE Homepage. Click CREATE MY ACCOUNT (top right of the navigation bar). Fill in your details to log in and schedule an appointment.

## When can I start using MDLIVE?

Immediately after creating your free account. You will only be asked for credit card information when you get care from a doctor or therapist. Be sure to fill out your medical history profile to better prepare your provider for your appointment.
