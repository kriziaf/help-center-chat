# Account Security & Privacy

## Is MDLIVE HIPAA compliant?

Yes. MDLIVE is fully compliant with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability (HIPAA) regulations. Your personal and medical information is protected under strict federal privacy and security laws.

## How secure is my personal health information?

All data is encrypted during transmission and storage. MDLIVE uses industry-standard security protocols and undergoes regular security audits. Your medical records are accessible only to you and your healthcare provider.

## Can I control who sees my medical records?

Yes. You have full control over your medical records. You can specify which providers can access your information and can request records be shared with other healthcare providers or family members.
