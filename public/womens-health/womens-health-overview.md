# Women's Health Overview & Care Options

## When can I get care? Do you have flexible appointments available?

MDLIVE provides on-demand care so you can talk to a doctor in just minutes for urgent needs, everyday care, or nagging issues. Or you can schedule an appointment at a time that works best for you. Whether in the middle of the night, after hours, or on a holiday, MD Live Urgent Care is available 24/7/365. If you prefer to get a diagnosis and treatment plan without talking to a doctor, E-Treatment is also available 24/7/365. Primary Care and Mental Health appointments are also available with flexible options, including evenings, weekends, and same-day appointments.* *Same-day appointments are not guaranteed and are subject to provider availability.

## How does an MD Live visit work?

Many medical conditions you seek in-person care can be effectively treated via video or phone. Your MD Live doctor will discuss your symptoms and provide a reliable diagnosis and treatment plan. Your doctor can order prescriptions to your preferred pharmacy and provide short-term renewals of existing medications. If follow-up care is needed, your doctor can refer you to a local, in-network PCP. With MD Live E-treatment, you'll complete an online interview about your symptoms for one of our board-certified doctors to review. The questionnaire takes about 8-10 minutes on average, and you'll get a diagnosis and treatment plan within an hour.

## Does insurance cover MD Live? What is the cost?

MD Live accepts many major insurances.* Depending on insurance, copays for participating plans may be as low as $0. Pricing is always shown upfront, so there are no surprise costs. Log in or create an account to view your coverage and copay details. *Not all insurance plans cover all MD Live services.

## Are there any age restrictions?

Urgent Care is available to children of all ages, from infants to teenagers. Conditions treated vary based on age. MD Live Mental Health cares for children and adolescents ages 10-17 with parent/guardian consent. MD Live Primary Care visits are only available for adults 18 and older.

## How can I trust MD Live doctors to provide the care I need?

MD Live doctors are board-certified, specially trained in telehealth care, and highly qualified, with an average of 15 years of experience in family, pediatric, emergency medicine, and urgent care. Our doctors accurately diagnose you by reviewing your health history, listening to your concerns, assessing your symptoms, and even reviewing photos or asking for your temperature and other vitals if needed. They determine the best treatment option — as if you were sitting inside a physical exam room with your doctor.

## Can I get care for a child or dependent?

Yes. Visits are available for children of all ages, from infants to teenagers, with board-certified pediatricians and family medicine doctors—available 24/7 on weekdays, weekends, and holidays. A parent or legal guardian must be present during the MD Live visit for any patient under 18. For mental health, MD Live treats children and adolescents ages 10-17 with parent/guardian consent.

## Can I use MD Live if I have a regular PCP or other doctor?

Yes. MD Live is a supplement to your existing doctors with fast, reliable care, so staying on top of your health from the comfort of your home is easier. Upon request, your MD Live doctor can share notes and results with your existing doctors so nothing is missed.

## How can MD Live help with perimenopause/menopause symptoms?

MDLIVE Primary Care doctors help women experiencing common symptoms of perimenopause and menopause. They can offer treatment recommendations, prescribe appropriate labs, anti-inflammatory/anti-oxidant resources, plan which may include hormone replacement therapy, weight management care, mental health support, and treatment for common symptoms such as sleep loss, hot flashes, etc.
