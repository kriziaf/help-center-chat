# Women's Health Screening & Prevention

## Can MDLIVE help coordinate mammograms?

Yes. MDLIVE doctors can order mammograms through coordinated telehealth screening and provide results review. If you're due for screening, your doctor can help schedule through local providers.

## What women's health screenings does MDLIVE recommend?

Age-appropriate screenings include mammograms (40+), cervical cancer screening (Pap smears, HPV testing), and preventive health assessments. Your doctor will recommend based on age and risk factors.

## How does MDLIVE coordinate cervical cancer screening?

MDLIVE can discuss screening options, recommend when you're due for a Pap smear or HPV test, and coordinate with local providers. Some tests may require in-person visits.

## Can I get contraceptive counseling through MDLIVE?

Yes. MDLIVE doctors discuss contraceptive options including birth control pills, patches, IUDs, implants, and permanent options. They help you choose the best method for your needs.

## Does MDLIVE provide HPV vaccination counseling?

Yes. Doctors can discuss HPV vaccination for cervical cancer prevention, who should be vaccinated, and coordinate vaccination through your local provider.

## What follow-up care is provided after screenings?

MDLIVE provides results review, next steps based on findings, and coordination of any needed treatment or referrals. Your doctor ensures continuity of care.
