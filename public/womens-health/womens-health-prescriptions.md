# Women's Health Prescriptions & Birth Control

## Can I get prescriptions?

If warranted, your MDLIVE doctors can order prescriptions to your preferred pharmacy and provide short-term renewals of existing medications. They can also view your health insurance information, determine covered medications, help you understand available medications and out-of-pocket costs, and prescribe the most appropriate option at their discretion.

## What forms of birth control can MDLIVE prescribe?

Birth control is available to pill, patch, and ring formats. Service is available for women 18-45 who are not currently pregnant and have no contraindications, at the physician's discretion.

## Can MDLIVE prescribe GLP-1s for weight management?

Yes. MDLIVE can prescribe GLP-1s and anti-obesity medications (e.g., Wegovy, Zepbound) as part of a weight management care plan at the physician's discretion. These medications are only available through a Routine Care visit — not during Primary Care Wellness Screening, Urgent Care, etc. — and are not available in certain states.
